In 1996, this council suspended voter identification, this council, because the parties could not agree on who should vote. In 1997, Secretary General Kofi Annan warned that the referendum may never happen at all. In 1999, Anan then reported that the prospect of holding a referendum was moving farther away, not closer. In 2002, Anan and his personal envoy, former Secretary of State James Baker, concluded that implementing the original settlement plan was, quote, “highly unlikely.” They even presented this council with the option of terminating MINURSO altogether as a result. In 2003, Anan warned that even if a referendum were held, The Security Council was highly unlikely to enforce its results. In '05, the director of MINURSO for five years, Eric Jensen, said that, quote, "The referendum called for the UN Security Council settlement plan of 1990-1991 has failed, will not happen."
